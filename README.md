# SimilarQuest — Module Documentation & Art Brief
**Grade 8 · Similarity Applications**

SimilarQuest is an interactive educational module teaching students how to decide whether figures are similar, find scale factors, solve for unknown lengths, and apply similarity to indirect measurement (shadows, mirrors, nested parallel lines, and scale drawings).

---

## Architecture & Technology Stack
- **Framework:** React 19 + Vite 8
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS + Vanilla CSS Design Tokens
- **Animations:** Framer Motion
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Audio Engine:** ElevenLabs Alice (`Xb7hH8MSUJpSbSDYk0k2`) with 6 emotional presets and zero browser TTS fallback
- **QA:** Question bank stress test (300 runs), independent arithmetic oracle, locale audit (G1)

---

## 5-Phase Learning Journey
1. **Wonder:** Interactive shadow stick puzzle with draggable sun and proportional shadow calculation.
2. **Story:** 4-panel survey narrative with Maya, Leo, and Echo the Bat measuring trees, photos, and cliffs.
3. **Simulate:** 4 archetype-driven interactive labs (no multiple choice questions):
   - *Station 1 (Zoom Lens Lab):* Concept discovery lab with live scale factor slider and additive growth toggle.
   - *Station 2 (Match the Model):* Build-to-target proportional scaling and orientation match.
   - *Station 3 (River Survey):* Multi-step composite construction across river obstacles.
   - *Station 4 (Replay Review):* Error-detective debugging surveyor misconceptions.
4. **Practice:** 10 themed survey worlds with 100 questions, 4/10 unlock rule, 4 practice modes, and 10 site bosses.
5. **Reflect:** Stepped 3-screen metacognitive check, journal prompts, and final scorecard.
